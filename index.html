Frequently Asked Questions About Typing Tests and Certificates

Can I get a free typing certificate for my job application or resume?

Yes, take our standardized 1-minute typing test, achieve your benchmark score, and download an official PDF certificate with a verifiable QR code and ID.

What typing speed is required for data entry and administrative jobs?

Most clerical, data entry, and administrative jobs require between 50 to 65 WPM with at least 95% accuracy. Medical transcriptionists and executive secretaries often require 70 to 85+ WPM.

How is Net WPM calculated?

Net WPM is calculated using the standard 5-character word formula: Gross WPM minus a 1-word deduction for every uncorrected error per minute.

How do employers verify my certificate?

Employers can enter the unique certificate ID into the TypeCert Verification Portal or scan the QR code printed on your certificate to inspect authenticated scores and timestamps.
